## Executive Summary

Second Brain Universe is a personal knowledge operating system that captures, processes, and preserves everything Albert learns, thinks, and believes. Unlike traditional note-taking tools, this is a **living system** designed for:

1. **Daily Operations** — Task management, project execution, client work
2. **Knowledge Management** — Ideas → insights → evergreen knowledge → cornerstone beliefs
3. **Legacy Building** — Intergenerational knowledge transfer to family and selective public sharing

**Architecture Evolution:** V1.0 Hub & Spoke → V4.0 4-Layer Universe with embedded PAIOS (Personal AI Operating System).

**Core Innovation:** Daily work IS knowledge creation — operating layer feeds knowledge layer weekly via DC-PIPE-013 extractor.
